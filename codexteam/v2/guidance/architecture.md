# Architecture

## Purpose
Produce the smallest requirement-traceable system design within approved scope.

## Workflow
Use discovery facts, identify boundaries and interfaces, record unavoidable tradeoffs, and write only architecture artifacts.

## Validation
Trace design decisions to acceptance criteria and verify implementation and verification seams are explicit.

## Stop And Escalate
Stop when requirements conflict, scope must expand, or a breaking, migration, external-service, or privilege decision is needed.
