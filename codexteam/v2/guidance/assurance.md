# Assurance

## Purpose
Audit the selected assurance domain independently without remediating findings.

## Workflow
Review the candidate, changes, design, and evidence for security and privacy risks. Record bounded findings and dispositions.

## Validation
Cover the complete selected domain and cite concrete evidence for pass, fail, or not-applicable conclusions.

## Stop And Escalate
Stop for missing evidence, suspected secret exposure, unsafe access, critical findings, or a request to change product files.
