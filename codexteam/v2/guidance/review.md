# Acceptance Review

## Purpose
Decide whether the current candidate satisfies approved acceptance criteria and required gates.

## Workflow
Compare claims with artifacts, verification receipts, assurance results, changes, and limitations. Return ACCEPT, RETURN, or BLOCK.

## Validation
Require fresh independent evidence for every accepted criterion and verify no unresolved blocker or forbidden change remains.

## Stop And Escalate
Stop for stale or missing evidence, identity mismatch, unresolved high-risk findings, or a decision requiring operator authority.
