# Discovery

## Purpose
Establish repository facts, approved scope, constraints, and acceptance gaps without changing product files.

## Workflow
Inspect only relevant project paths. Separate observed facts from assumptions. Return a concise, traceable discovery report.

## Validation
Confirm every claim has a path, contract, or command source and every acceptance criterion is addressed.

## Stop And Escalate
Stop for missing authority, unsafe paths, credentials, contradictory requirements, or a material product decision.
