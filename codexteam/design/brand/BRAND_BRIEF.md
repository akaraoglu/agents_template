# CodexTeam Brand Brief

## Purpose

Create a monochrome identity for CodexTeam that communicates bounded agent
orchestration, deliberate handoffs, and verified delivery. The mark must work in
the operational Web UI without competing with task-status colors.

## Audience And Context

The primary audience is a technical operator supervising multiple projects,
tasks, agents, gates, and local commits. The identity appears most often as a
small Web UI header mark and favicon, with a wider banner reserved for project
documentation and presentation surfaces.

## Required Qualities

- Minimal, technical, quiet, and precise.
- Recognizable at `16`, `32`, `64`, and `128 px`.
- Exactly two brand colors, with automatic light/dark inversion.
- Distinct from the EnerGIT branch emblem and the OpenAI logomark.
- No gradients, shadows, glow, transparency, mascots, sparkles, brains, chat
  bubbles, or generic robot imagery.

## Palette

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coal | `#191B1F` | Light-theme mark, wordmark, and dark field |
| Mist | `#F2F3F5` | Dark-theme mark, wordmark, and light field |

Neutral grays may label a design sheet but are not part of the logo.

## Design Criteria

1. One clear metaphor rather than a collection of agent symbols.
2. A stable silhouette before internal meaning.
3. Pixel-aligned geometry and consistent stroke weight.
4. No more than four meaningful internal elements at favicon scale.
5. The `CodexTeam` wordmark remains readable without the emblem.
6. The banner must remain a brand asset, not a marketing hero inside the Web UI.

## Research Basis

- Apple icon guidance prioritizes one recognizable, simplified concept and
  consistency of weight and perspective.
- GNOME symbolic guidance uses monochrome vectors, a `16 x 16` nominal grid,
  pixel alignment, and strong main strokes.
- IBM icon guidance uses a fixed master grid, consistent strokes, safe padding,
  straight-on geometry, and optical centering.
- OpenAI brand guidance makes its marks proprietary; CodexTeam must not borrow
  the OpenAI knot, wordmark construction, or endorsement language.

## Production Boundary

This study is isolated under `design/brand/`. No Web UI source or packaged asset
changes until the selected direction is approved.
